🌳 Tree of Life

Each band is a species — a cluster of genetically similar creatures — and its thickness is that species' share of the pond, not its headcount: every column is exactly full, so a band can widen while the population falls. Watch lineages appear, sweep to dominance, and go extinct. Click a named one to spotlight it in the pond above; the stippled band along the bottom is the churn of lineages too small to earn a name, and there is nothing behind it to spotlight. The plot always spans the whole run: as the run grows it gets coarser, never shorter.
